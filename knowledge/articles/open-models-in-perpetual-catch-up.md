Critical analysis of the gap between open and closed AI language models, examining claims made by Chinese models and the realistic trajectory of open model development. The article explores the open-closed gap, distillation techniques, innovation timescales, how open models can win, specialized models, and what capabilities are still missing from open alternatives.

## Key Takeaways

- Open models are in perpetual catch-up to closed models like Claude Opus and GPT-4
- Many claims about Chinese models matching expensive proprietary models are overstated
- Distillation techniques allow open models to leverage insights from larger models
- Innovation speed in the AI industry varies between open and closed approaches
- Specialized open models show promise in specific domains despite general capability gaps
- Realistic assessment of strengths and weaknesses is essential for adoption decisions
