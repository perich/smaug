Simon Sarris discusses his personal audiobook recommendations, making the case that audiobooks are legitimate books and also serve as a form of practice for listeners. The article explores how consuming content through audio can be both a reading experience and a skill-building activity.

## Key Takeaways

- Audiobooks are a valid form of reading and book consumption
- Listening to audiobooks can serve as a practice activity for developing skills
- Individual preferences determine which format works best for different types of learners
