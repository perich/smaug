A founder's journey building an entirely stainless steel coffee maker as an environmentally conscious alternative to traditional brewing equipment. After quitting his job three months prior, JC Foster is developing an affordable, convenient, and completely plastic-free drip-style coffee maker where every component—tank, valves, showerhead, pipe, and body—is made from stainless steel.

The product addresses a significant gap in the market where most coffee makers contain plastic components that degrade with heat and chemicals, potentially affecting water quality and beverage taste. Puresteel's all-steel construction ensures durability, safety, and environmental sustainability.

## Key Takeaways

- Every component is made from stainless steel for durability and sustainability
- Designed as an affordable alternative to traditional plastic-laden coffee makers
- Community-driven development with pre-orders supporting the entrepreneurial venture
- Focuses on quality materials and longevity over disposable consumption
