# Synthesizing Marketing Copy with AI: The Try Homepage Approach

**Source:** Tobi Lütke (Shopify)

A practical workflow for using multiple AI models to create marketing content by combining sentiment analysis with copywriting.

## The Workflow

1. **Sentiment Analysis Phase (Grok)**
   - Aggregate what people are saying about the product on social media (X/Twitter)
   - Synthesize key themes and audience sentiment
   - Identify value propositions resonating with users

2. **Copywriting Phase (Claude)**
   - Take the synthesized insights
   - Transform into compelling marketing pitch
   - Generate homepage copy

## Key Insight

**Divide and conquer with AI** - Different AI models have different strengths:
- **Grok** - Real-time data analysis, social listening, synthesis
- **Claude** - Creative copywriting, narrative structure, persuasion

## Advantages

- **Data-Driven** - Messaging grounded in actual user sentiment, not speculation
- **Efficient** - Faster than traditional market research → copywriting cycle
- **Iterative** - Easy to refine both phases based on feedback
- **Fun** - Demonstrates AI as a collaborative tool, not just a code generator

## Applications

Beyond homepages:
- Product positioning statements
- Feature descriptions
- Marketing campaigns
- Value propositions
- Taglines and headlines

## Tools Used

- Grok (real-time X data analysis)
- Claude (natural language generation and copywriting)

## Philosophy

This reflects a broader shift where **AI becomes a strategic tool** for marketing and product positioning, not just for technical tasks. It's collaborative: humans still define direction and taste, but AI handles synthesis and generation.
