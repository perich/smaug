Situation Monitor is a comprehensive real-time monitoring dashboard that aggregates activity and information streams from multiple domains into a unified interface.

## Features

### Data Streams
- **Global Activity Monitor**: Real-time geopolitical and world events
- **Intel Feed**: Current intelligence and news feeds
- **Tech/Finance/Politics Newsfeed**: Cross-domain news aggregation
- **Market Data**: Live stocks and cryptocurrency tracking
- **Polymarket Predictions**: Real-time prediction market data
- **Tech Layoffs Tracker**: Employment changes in tech industry
- **AI Race News**: Latest developments in AI competition
- **Fed Operations**: Monetary policy and printer status ("Is the Fed printer on?")
- **Geopolitical Hotspots**: Focused tracking of Venezuela and Greenland situations

## Use Cases

- Situational awareness across interconnected systems
- Understanding relationships between geopolitics, finance, and technology
- Monitoring emerging trends in AI and tech
- Real-time decision support for policy and investment

## Design Philosophy

Situation Monitor operates on the principle that modern information environments are interconnected—what happens in geopolitics affects markets, which affects tech hiring, which affects AI development. A unified dashboard enables pattern recognition across these normally siloed domains.

## Technical

Built with Vercel for real-time deployment and streaming updates. Integrates multiple data sources and APIs to maintain fresh information.
