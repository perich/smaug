A critical examination of traditional logging approaches and their inherent limitations in providing reliable observability for systems.

## Overview

This article challenges conventional wisdom about logging practices and proposes wide events as a superior alternative for system observability and debugging.

## Key Arguments

### Traditional Logging Failures
- Log systems often provide incomplete or misleading information
- Context is frequently lost in conventional logging approaches
- Wide range of data needed for effective debugging is absent from traditional logs

### Wide Events Solution
- Proposes structured events with rich contextual information
- Comprehensive data capture at the time of events
- Better support for debugging and observability

## Implications

The article is relevant for:
- System administrators and DevOps engineers
- Application developers building observable systems
- Teams struggling with debugging production issues
- Those evaluating observability tools and approaches
