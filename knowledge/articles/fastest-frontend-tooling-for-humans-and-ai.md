A comprehensive guide to modern frontend tooling optimizations for 2026, focusing on speed improvements for both human developers and AI systems. 2026 is the year JavaScript tooling gets faster with TypeScript being rewritten in Go and tools like Oxlint and Oxfmt becoming mainstream. The article advocates for fast feedback loops, strict guardrails, and strong local reasoning to benefit both developers and LLMs.

## Key Takeaways

- TypeScript is being rewritten in Go (tsgo) for 10x faster type checking
- Oxlint and Oxfmt are production-ready replacements for ESLint and Prettier
- Fast feedback loops and strict guardrails improve both human and AI code generation
- pnpm and Vite remain excellent tools for package management and bundling
- Multiple templates provided for web, mobile, library, and server projects
