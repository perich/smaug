# Local SEO: Common Mistakes and Quick Wins

## Overview
Comprehensive guide to the 20+ most common local SEO mistakes found in business audits, with actionable fixes that can dramatically improve rankings and lead generation.

## Key Mistakes & Fixes

### Meta Tags & Content
- **Meta title says "Home"** → Change to keyword + city (e.g., "AC Repair San Diego")
- **H1 tag says "Welcome to Our Company"** → Use service + city instead
- **No schema markup** → Add business name, address, hours (easy win)

### Google Business Profile (GBP)
- **Wrong category** → Match top 3 competitors; can double calls
- **No photos in 6+ months** → Upload weekly (job sites, team, truck photos)
- **Generic review responses** → Add keywords and location mentions
- **No GBP posts in 3 months** → Weekly updates with photos and keywords (5 min task)
- **Only one GBP when multiple locations available** → Get offices in different metro areas to triple coverage

### Citation & NAP Consistency
- **Wrong/inconsistent phone numbers** → Fix on YellowPages, Yelp, 40+ sites
- **No address in website footer** → Google wants to verify location on your site

### Content & Pages
- **No location pages** → Build pages for each service area (serve 15 cities? 15 pages)
- **Blog topics nobody searches** → Focus on buyer intent content, not history/background
- **Not on niche directories** → Plumbers need plumber directories; HVAC needs HVAC directories

### Technical
- **Website loads in 8+ seconds on mobile** → Fix page speed or rankings mean nothing
- **No call tracking** → Can't measure leads or ROI; install call tracking

### Signals & Engagement
- **Employees not using Google Maps to navigate** → Engagement signals matter; every navigation counts
- **Ignoring Reddit** → City subreddits have daily recommendation requests
- **Relying on service area business without physical pin** → Get a real address; service-only can't win
